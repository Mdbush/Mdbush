# Client Proposal Pack (UAE & GCC)

**Win more clients with proposals that sell for you.** Five ready-to-use
templates that turn enquiries into signed clients — for freelancers and
solopreneurs across the UAE and the wider GCC. Fill in the brackets, send, and
follow up on autopilot.

**How to use:** everything in `[BRACKETS]` is yours to fill in. Lead with the
client's problem and the outcome you deliver — never with your CV.

---

## 1. The Discovery-Call Script

Run this **before** you quote. A good discovery call wins the deal before the
proposal is written.

**Open (2 min):** "Thanks for the time, `[NAME]`. So I can make this useful —
walk me through what's going on and what made you reach out now?"

**Qualify (the 5 questions):**
1. "What does success look like 90 days after this is done?"
2. "What's this costing you today — in time, money, or missed opportunities?"
3. "Is there a budget range you're working within?"
4. "Who else is involved in the decision to go ahead?"
5. "What happened with the last person/agency you tried this with?"

**Close the call:** "Based on this, here's roughly how I'd approach it: `[1-2
SENTENCES]`. I'll send a short proposal by `[DAY]` with options. Sound good?"

> **Rule:** never quote a number live on the first call. Buy yourself the
> proposal.

## 2. The Winning Proposal Template

Keep it to one page. Structure beats length.

**Proposal for `[CLIENT]`** — prepared by `[YOUR NAME]`, `[DATE]`

**The problem.** `[RESTATE THEIR PROBLEM IN THEIR WORDS — 2-3 SENTENCES. THIS IS
THE MOST IMPORTANT PARAGRAPH.]`

**The outcome.** After this project you'll have `[THE RESULT, NOT THE TASK — e.g.
"a website that turns visitors into booked calls," not "a 5-page website"]`.

**How we'll get there.**
- `[PHASE 1 — e.g. Discovery & strategy]`
- `[PHASE 2 — e.g. Design & build]`
- `[PHASE 3 — e.g. Launch & handover]`

**Timeline.** `[N]` weeks from deposit. Estimated start: `[DATE]`.

**Investment.** See the options below. All prices in AED, one-time unless stated.

**Next step.** Reply "let's go" and I'll send the agreement and invoice for the
50% deposit. Your slot is held until `[DATE]`.

## 3. Pricing & Packages One-Pager

Always give **three** options. Most clients pick the middle one — so price the
middle one where you actually want to land.

| | **Essential** | **Recommended** ⭐ | **Complete** |
|---|---|---|---|
| For | `[WHO]` | `[WHO]` | `[WHO]` |
| Includes | `[SCOPE]` | Essential + `[MORE]` | Recommended + `[MORE]` |
| Timeline | `[N] wks` | `[N] wks` | `[N] wks` |
| **Investment** | `AED [LOW]` | `AED [MID]` | `AED [HIGH]` |

> Anchor high: listing the Complete tier first makes Recommended feel like the
> sensible choice. Add a line: *"Most clients choose Recommended."*

## 4. Pitch Email Templates

**Cold (a specific person at a company you'd love to work with):**

> Subject: quick idea for `[COMPANY]`
>
> Hi `[NAME]` — I noticed `[SPECIFIC OBSERVATION ABOUT THEIR BUSINESS]`.
> I help `[WHO]` `[ACHIEVE OUTCOME]`; recently I `[ONE-LINE PROOF WITH A NUMBER]`.
> Worth a 15-minute call to see if I can do the same for `[COMPANY]`?
> — `[YOUR NAME]`, `[LINK]`

**Warm (a referral or past contact):**

> Subject: `[REFERRER]` suggested I reach out
>
> Hi `[NAME]` — `[REFERRER]` mentioned you're looking at `[PROJECT]`. That's
> exactly what I do for `[WHO]`. Here's a recent example: `[PROOF]`. Free
> `[DAY]` for a quick call?

## 5. The 3-Email Follow-Up Sequence

~60% of proposals close in the follow-up, not the send. Never "just checking in."

**Email 1 — 3 days after sending:**
> Subject: your `[PROJECT]` proposal
> Hi `[NAME]`, wanted to make sure this reached you and see if any questions came
> up. Happy to jump on a quick call to walk through the options.

**Email 2 — 7 days, add value:**
> Hi `[NAME]`, while this is on your desk — one quick idea: `[A SPECIFIC,
> USEFUL SUGGESTION FOR THEIR PROBLEM]`. It's the kind of thing we'd tackle
> together. Still keen to move forward?

**Email 3 — 12 days, the close:**
> Hi `[NAME]`, I'm holding your start slot until `[DATE]`, after which my
> schedule opens to new clients. Happy to adjust scope or terms if that helps.
> What's the status on your end?

> After Email 3, stop. Mark the lead "nurture" and follow up in 90 days.
