# Freelance Contract Kit (UAE & GCC)

**Contracts that actually get you paid.** Five ready-to-use templates plus a
clause library, written in plain English for freelancers and solopreneurs across
the UAE and the wider GCC. Fill in the brackets, export to PDF, send for
signature — and collect your deposit before you start.

> **Not legal advice.** These are general templates suitable for typical freelance
> work. They are editable starting points, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
> For high-value, cross-border, or disputed contracts, have a UAE-licensed lawyer
> review your final version.

**How to use:** every field in `[BRACKETS]` is yours to fill in. Delete any clause
that doesn't apply. Keep the payment, late-fee, and IP clauses — they're the ones
that protect you.

---

## 1. Freelance Service Agreement

Use this for a defined project with a clear deliverable — fixed-price or hourly.

**THIS AGREEMENT** is made on `[DATE]` between:

- **The Freelancer:** `[YOUR NAME / TRADING NAME]`, `[EMIRATE, UAE]` ("the Freelancer")
- **The Client:** `[CLIENT NAME / COMPANY]`, `[LOCATION]` ("the Client")

**1. Services.** The Freelancer will provide: `[DESCRIBE THE SERVICES / DELIVERABLES]`,
as further defined in the attached Scope of Work.

**2. Fees.**
- Fixed price: `AED [AMOUNT]` for the full scope; **or**
- Hourly: `AED [RATE]/hour`, estimated `[N]` hours, billed `[weekly/monthly]`.

**3. Payment terms.**
- A **non-refundable deposit of 50%** (`AED [AMOUNT]`) is due before work begins.
- The balance is due `[on delivery / within 7 days of invoice]`.
- Invoices unpaid after `[14]` days incur a **late fee of 2% of the outstanding
  amount per week** (see Clause Library).

**4. Timeline.** Work begins on `[START DATE]` once the deposit is received.
Estimated delivery: `[DATE / DURATION]`. Timelines assume the Client provides
required materials and feedback within `[3]` business days.

**5. Revisions.** The fee includes `[2]` rounds of revisions. Additional revisions
are billed at `AED [RATE]/hour` or a flat `AED [AMOUNT]` per round.

**6. Intellectual property.** Ownership of the final deliverables transfers to the
Client **only upon full payment**. Until then, all work remains the property of the
Freelancer. The Freelancer may display the work in their portfolio unless the
Client requests otherwise in writing.

**7. Independent contractor.** The Freelancer is an independent contractor, not an
employee, and is responsible for their own visa, permits, and taxes.

**8. Termination.** Either party may terminate with `[7]` days' written notice. On
termination the Client pays for all work completed to date; the deposit is
non-refundable.

**9. Confidentiality.** Both parties will keep each other's non-public information
confidential (see the NDA in Section 4 for a standalone version).

**10. Governing terms.** This Agreement is governed by the laws applicable at the
Client's place of business unless otherwise agreed in writing.

**Signed:**
`[FREELANCER]` __________ Date ______   `[CLIENT]` __________ Date ______

## 2. Scope of Work + Change Request

Attach to the Service Agreement so "scope creep" has somewhere to go.

**Project:** `[NAME]`  **Date:** `[DATE]`

**In scope (deliverables):**
- `[DELIVERABLE 1]`
- `[DELIVERABLE 2]`
- `[DELIVERABLE 3]`

**Explicitly out of scope:** `[LIST ANYTHING THE CLIENT MIGHT ASSUME IS INCLUDED]`

**Milestones & payments:**
| Milestone | Deliverable | % of fee | Due |
|-----------|-------------|----------|-----|
| Kickoff   | Deposit     | 50%      | Before start |
| `[M2]`    | `[…]`       | `[25%]`  | `[DATE]` |
| Delivery  | Final files | 25%      | On approval |

**Change request.** Any work outside the "In scope" list requires a signed change
request:

> Requested change: `[DESCRIBE]`. Additional cost: `AED [AMOUNT]`. Additional time:
> `[N days]`. Approved by `[CLIENT]` on `[DATE]`.

## 3. Monthly Retainer Agreement

For ongoing work — content, maintenance, advisory, marketing.

- **Retainer:** `AED [AMOUNT]/month` for `[SCOPE — e.g. up to 20 hours or the
  following deliverables]`.
- **Billing:** invoiced on the `[1st]` of each month, **payable in advance**.
- **Rollover:** unused hours `[do / do not]` roll over. Excess hours billed at
  `AED [RATE]/hour`.
- **Term:** minimum `[3]` months, then rolling monthly.
- **Notice:** `[30]` days' written notice to cancel. The current month is not
  refundable.
- **Pause:** the Client may pause for up to `[1]` month per year with `[14]` days'
  notice.

## 4. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

Two versions — pick one.

**One-way NDA** (they share confidential info with you):
The Receiving Party (`[FREELANCER]`) agrees to keep confidential all non-public
information disclosed by `[CLIENT]`, to use it only for the project, and to return
or destroy it on request. Obligations last `[2]` years from disclosure. Excludes
information that is public, already known, or independently developed.

**Mutual NDA** (both sides share): as above, but obligations apply equally to both
parties for information each discloses.

## 5. Clause Library — copy the ones that protect you

**Deposit (non-negotiable):**
> "A non-refundable deposit of 50% is required before work commences. Work is
> scheduled only once the deposit is received."

**Late payment:**
> "Invoices not settled within 14 days of the due date incur a late fee of 2% of
> the outstanding balance per week. Work may be paused on any account more than 14
> days overdue until the balance is cleared."

**Kill fee (project cancelled mid-way):**
> "If the Client cancels after work has begun, the Client pays for all work
> completed plus a kill fee of 25% of the remaining project value."

**Scope creep:**
> "Requests outside the agreed Scope of Work will be quoted separately and require a
> signed change request before the additional work begins."

**Feedback / delay:**
> "Timelines assume feedback within 3 business days. Delays in feedback or materials
> extend the delivery date by the length of the delay."

**IP on payment:**
> "Ownership of deliverables transfers to the Client only upon receipt of full
> payment. Until then, all rights remain with the Freelancer."

**Portfolio rights:**
> "The Freelancer may display completed work in their portfolio and case studies
> unless the Client opts out in writing."
